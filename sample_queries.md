# Sample Queries

## Basic Queries

- Show all customers.
- Show all customers from Pune.
- List all products.
- Show products with price greater than 1000.

## Aggregation

- What is the average order value?
- What is the total revenue?
- Show the top 5 customers by total spending.

## Joins

- Show customer names and their orders.
- Show products included in each order.
- Show total spending for each customer.

## Sorting and Limiting

- Show the 5 most expensive products.
- Show the latest 5 orders.
- Show the top 3 customers by number of orders.

## Blocked Queries

These should be rejected:

- Delete all customers.
- Update customer cities.
- Insert a new customer.
- Drop the customers table.
- Truncate the orders table.
